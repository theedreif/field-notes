> # When the plan stops working, the mission does not stop with it.

Field Notes is where doctrine meets reality.

This repository connects ideas from **Operational Readiness & Mission Success** to the environments in which people actually have to perform: changing conditions, incomplete information, artificial intelligence, autonomous platforms, communication gaps, time pressure, and consequences.

The recurring operating problem is simple:

> **The technology changes. The operating problem does not: somebody still has to recognize when reality has departed from the plan.**

## The Field Library

> # BUILD READINESS → SCALE JUDGMENT → OPERATE AUTONOMY → COMMUNICATE UNDER CONSEQUENCE

### BOOK 1 — PREPARE
## THIS IS NOT TRAINING
**Build capability that survives contact with reality.**

The foundation: scenario is the product, friction is the mechanism, and readiness must be demonstrated through behavior and evidence rather than completion alone.

### BOOK 2 — SCALE
## Me, Myself and AI
**Turn scarce human judgment into scalable operational capability.**

When AI makes content abundant, expert judgment remains scarce. The operational challenge becomes extracting, encoding, and scaling what experienced practitioners know.

### BOOK 3 — OPERATE
## What To Expect When You Are Expecting Drones
**Keep the mission alive when autonomy stops behaving autonomously.**

The human role moves beyond simple operation toward orchestration, diagnosis, intervention, and command. The **Optineer** is the operator-engineer prepared to act when autonomous behavior no longer matches the mission.

**[Listen on Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/show/6r2GI9oDPEOz5zloBijDlR)**

### BOOK 4 — COMMUNICATE
## We Speak English or People Die
**Close the gap between what was said, understood, and done.**

High-consequence communication lives in the dangerous distance between what somebody says, what another person hears, what they understand, and what they actually do next.

## From Doctrine to Field Evidence

The books establish the ideas. Field Notes asks what happens when those ideas encounter actual conditions.

Areas of investigation include:

- operational readiness and field learning;
- human judgment under uncertainty;
- artificial intelligence and expertise;
- human-machine teaming;
- autonomous aerial and maritime systems;
- Drone as First Responder operations;
- robotic warfare and the changing operator role;
- high-consequence communication;
- decision evidence and after-action learning.
