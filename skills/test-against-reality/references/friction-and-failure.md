# Friction and Failure

Friction reveals where a model encounters resistance.

Look for:
- ambiguous signals;
- missing information;
- competing priorities;
- time compression;
- resource loss;
- degraded systems;
- handoff failures;
- communication gaps;
- unexpected automation;
- authority boundaries;
- human workload;
- changed mission conditions.

## Failure is diagnostic

Ask whether the failure came from:
- doctrine;
- design;
- implementation;
- execution;
- environment;
- assumption;
- measurement;
- communication;
- human-machine interaction.

Avoid labeling every bad outcome a framework failure. Equally, avoid explaining away repeated contradictions merely to preserve the framework.
