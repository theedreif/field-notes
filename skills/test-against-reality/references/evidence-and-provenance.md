# Evidence and Provenance

Keep the evidence trail visible.

## Evidence status

**DOCUMENTED** — directly supported by a source or observation.

**REPORTED** — stated by a participant or source but not independently verified.

**INFERRED** — reasoned from available evidence.

**HYPOTHESIS** — plausible but insufficiently tested.

**PROPOSED** — a recommendation or future design choice.

**UNKNOWN** — insufficient evidence.

## Provenance record

For important claims capture:
- source;
- date or version when relevant;
- context;
- what the source actually supports;
- interpretation added later;
- uncertainty;
- conflicting evidence.

Do not make a source carry more weight than its context permits.
