# After-Action Learning

Separate the decision moment from what became known later.

## THEN
What was known?
What signals were visible?
What was uncertain?
What limits applied?
What decision was made?
Why?

## NOW
What happened?
What new evidence appeared?
Which assumptions held?
Which failed?
What was surprising?

## NEXT
What changes?
What remains uncertain?
What should be tested?
What should an operator notice sooner?
What evidence should be captured next time?

The objective is not blame or storytelling. It is a better next iteration.
